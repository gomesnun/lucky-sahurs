What's in the box

A full idle-game loop, hand built in Python + pygame.

Pet gacha

11 rarity tiers, from a 1-in-2 Common up to a 1-in-500-billion Transcendent. Every pet has its own income and a shot at a rare mutation.

Rebirths

Cash out your run for permanent money and luck multipliers, then start again stronger — the core loop that keeps every run escalating.

Upgrade tree

Sink earnings into money, luck and auto-roll-speed upgrades that scale your whole run, level by level.

Shop

New stock every 10 minutes, the same for everyone: 10 dice that give permanent luck (and restyle your ROLL button) and Coin, Speed and Luck potions.

Traits

12 equippable traits, from a common Lucky to the 1-in-8-million Omnipotent, reshaping money, luck and mutation odds.

Milestones

11 long-term tracks — rolls, coins earned, playtime and more — each handing out permanent bonuses as you climb.

Online leaderboard

Sign in to sync your save to the cloud and see how your stash stacks up against everyone else.

Friends

Search a player by username, send a friend request and follow their stats — with any Verity you own as your profile photo, Golden and Diamond ring included.

Daily missions

Fresh objectives every day, paid out in trait charges — always a reason to open the game.

Cloud saves

Three local save slots plus optional account sync, so your progress survives a reinstall or a new machine.

Self-updating

The built executable checks GitHub on launch (and every 30 minutes) and offers to update — never blocking without a confirmed newer version.

Offline earnings

Come back to 30% of your income for up to 8 hours away — upgradeable on both fronts.

The full pet index

Every tier, every pet, and the base rarity behind it — straight from the game's own tables.

✨ Golden mutation

3× income · up to a 25% chance per roll once unlocked and fully upgraded.

💎 Diamond mutation

9× income · up to a 9% chance per roll once unlocked and fully upgraded — always rarer than Golden.

🌈 Rainbow mutation

27× income · the rarest mutation, unlocked late in the upgrade tree.

Traits

Rolled with trait charges earned from regular rolls. Only the equipped trait grants its buffs — rarer traits stack money, luck, mutation and auto-roll-speed bonuses at once.

Long-term progression

The numbers behind rebirths and milestones.

Rebirths

  • 500,000 coins for the first rebirth, ×3.2 cost each time after
  • +7% money/sec and +4% luck on Rare+ pets, per rebirth, forever
  • Unlocks the Auto Roller at 2 rebirths

Milestones

11 tracked stats, each with up to 15 tiers of permanent rewards:

Stack it all

  • Upgrades, traits, milestones and rebirths all multiply together
  • A Golden, Diamond and Rainbow Roll bonus can line up at once — luck adds, it doesn't just multiply one bonus
  • Fully maxed: 25% Golden chance, 9% Diamond chance, every rarity boosted

How to play

Mouse-only rolling, keyboard shortcuts for everything else.

U Upgrades I Index M Milestones B Bag F11 Fullscreen ESC Back / close

Side buttons open Index / Upgrades / Milestones / Daily on the right and Bag / Rebirth / Traits on the left. The top bar covers Stats and Options — including the Windows default / Dark / Light theme switch and the English ⇄ Português toggle this page borrows the labels from.
